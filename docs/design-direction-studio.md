# NextGridIT — technical studio direction

## Brief

Audience: local business owners and leaders in Upstate South Carolina who need
networks, cabling, Microsoft 365, security assessments, or help taking ownership
of their IT. Main action: book the existing Microsoft consultation; email and
phone remain available. Preserve every public route, substantive service scope,
metadata, resource, API behavior, and saved theme preference.

The existing site is usable but too text-heavy and anonymous. The new argument:
IT should stop interrupting the business; NextGridIT connects the physical and
digital pieces, makes the risks understandable, and delivers a documented handoff.
Show this through an original infrastructure illustration, a practical service
selector, a real sanitized project, and Benjamin's documented experience.

## Explorations

Three working hero compositions are compared in a local-only prototype:

- Editorial studio: light ground, large human-language headline, dark hardware
  illustration, and clear green action. Balances approachability and expertise.
- Infrastructure workshop: dark full-width ground, technical framing and the
  hardware as the main focal point. More dramatic, but less approachable for a
  nontechnical owner.
- Independent consultant: type-led composition with a personal introduction
  and a project excerpt. Warmer but lacks a strong visual centerpiece without
  original portrait photography.

Selected after viewing all three at 1440px and the editorial version at 390px:
Editorial studio. The dark workshop made the opening feel too technical; the
personal direction without photography gave the monogram too much weight.
Editorial retains a strong visual subject and a human headline. The local
prototype exercised all three at both widths without document overflow.
Prototype controls are not production content.

## Visual system

Pine green remains the recognizable brand and the normal light-theme button
color remains #17633d. Use mineral white, dark pine, warm silver imagery, and
limited chartreuse highlights. Existing Space Grotesk carries interfaces and
body text; a single editorial italic face gives key phrases a human voice.
A wide but bounded layout, varied section compositions, large service rows,
clear reading measures and tactile illustrations replace repeated small cards.

No invented testimonials, partners, client photography, ratings, or credentials.
The generated hero is an illustration, labeled as such; it does not document a
client installation. Actual portrait and installation photography can be added
when available without blocking a complete design.

Motion: preserve usable native scrolling and reduced-motion preferences. Menu
transitions, one-time supporting-section arrivals, and restrained hover/press
feedback support the composition. No endless hardware movement or fake live
network activity. The service selector uses real buttons, keyboard navigation,
and links to the actual services, with no pretend monitoring data.

## References

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Oxide (https://oxide.computer/) demonstrates showing tangible
infrastructure alongside ownership-oriented explanations. Tailscale
(https://tailscale.com/) demonstrates organizing technical capabilities around
visitor use cases. These are principles, not assets or copied layouts. Local
peer comparisons are documented in the preceding conversation.
